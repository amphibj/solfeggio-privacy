Privacy Policy for Solfeggio Frequencies

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

Introduction

Solfeggio Frequencies ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Solfeggio Frequencies is designed with privacy in mind:

  • No account required - Use the app without creating an account
  • No data collection - We do not collect usage data or analytics
  • No internet connection required - The app works entirely offline
  • No third-party services - We do not integrate with external services
  • No advertising - We do not display ads or use ad networks
  • No location tracking - We do not access or track your location

Information Stored Locally

The app stores the following information only on your device:

  • Audio settings (volume level, selected waveform)
  • Pure tone generator frequency preference

This data never leaves your device and is used solely to remember your preferences between app sessions.

Audio and Microphone

  • Audio Playback: The app generates audio tones locally on your device for healing and meditation purposes.
  • Microphone: We do not access your microphone or camera.
  • Background Audio: The app can continue playing audio when minimized, with your permission via iOS settings.

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on this page with an updated date.
